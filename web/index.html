Hand-tracking fireworks

Shoot flowers with your fingers

  1. 👉Make a finger gun: index out, other fingers curled, thumb up
  2. 👍Snap your thumb down like a trigger
  3. 🌷Watch it burst into a tulip, sunflower, rose, lily or ring — both hands work

Your camera is processed on this device only — nothing is uploaded. The first start downloads the hand-tracking model (about 12 MB), then it is cached.

How to play

  1. ✋Keep your whole hand in view, in good light
  2. 👉Hold a finger gun until the dot on your fingertip glows
  3. 👍Lift your thumb, then snap it down to fire

Tip: press Space or double-tap the screen to fire a test shot.
